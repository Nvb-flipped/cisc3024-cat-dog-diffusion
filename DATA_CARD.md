# Data card — provisional pending the official audit

**Task.** 32×32 RGB photographs of one centered, roughly upright cat/dog head, ears included; 60–80% head occupancy, original head width ≥32 px. Labels: cat=0, dog=1. Training targets must be exact manifest crops of the supplied course pool. Data stay local and course-only.

**Final training manifest: `manifests/v014.csv` (= `submission/manifest.csv`).** 8,029 exact crops (3,096 cat, 4,933 dog) from 7,719 distinct pool images, plus a 63-crop pool-only holdout (31 cat, 32 dog) that is byte-identical to the v009/v010 holdout. 1,929 rows are the student's personally reviewed crops (v010, 880 cat and 1,049 dog; 50 reviewed batches). 6,100 rows were found by AI-assisted curation of the rest of the pool and accepted by an **agent visual review**, not by the student. The student reported a TA clarification that AI-assisted cleaning qualifies; this is a student report, and any written confirmation should be kept with the submission. The official 200-crop audit remains the student's own personal task.

**Pipeline.** Delivered-coordinate RGB decoding → detector and geometry proposals → student review (v010) and AI-assisted screening (CLIP, Qwen3.5 vision-language model) → agent visual review under the exact specification → duplicate-family removal → immutable six-column manifest → official reconstruction. The curation models (Grounding DINO Tiny, CLIP ViT-B/32, Qwen3.5-4B and 9B) were used only to curate pool crops. The evaluator's classifier and Inception network, dev images and dev features were never used for curation, training or generation. No EXIF reorientation, padding, generatively edited targets or external training images. Training uses only exact manifest crops. The one augmentation is whole-crop left–right mirroring with probability 0.5 (the evaluator counts mirrors as copies in its memorization test; mirrored images are never added as manifest rows). The generator trains from random initialization.

**Quality and uncertainty.** Species labels for AI-found crops come from the vision-language model and were cross-checked with a CLIP zero-shot classifier on all 6,366 AI rows of the first freeze: 11 disagreed (0.17%, none strongly), and visual inspection found all 11 VLM labels correct. A random sample of 96 of the 5,206 AI rows that the one-head scan had not flagged held no second head and 3 non-photos (a painting, a figurine, a print on a mug; about 3%, 95% Wilson interval 1–9%); those three were removed. The main review used contact-sheet thumbnails and the re-checks used larger tiles, so some residual errors (for example ear tips at the edge of the square, small graphics, non-photo renderings) are likely; their real rate is unknown until the student's audit. These estimates come from agent inspection, not from student judgement. Audit precision and its Wilson interval await the student labels.

**Limitations.** Agent verdicts are not student verdicts and not independent of the screening models' selection of what to show. The review rule was relaxed from a stricter early rule to the exact specification after the stricter data gave lower recall (decision log entries 49–50). The 166 personal crops whose preview hashes were verified only by deterministic re-rendering on Linux are listed in `results/curation/preview_portability_20261119.json`.
