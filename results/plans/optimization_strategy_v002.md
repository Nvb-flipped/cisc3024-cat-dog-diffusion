# Optimization strategy for the reviewed pool

The corrected v002 noise-prediction reference scored 11/15 on each of three full development evaluations. It passed the hard checks with 4.10–4.60% memorization. Accuracy was 89.40–89.95%, FID 71.59–72.80 and recall 30.20–32.30%. The excellent tier requires accuracy ≥90%, FID ≤50 and recall ≥40%, with memorization ≤5% and every other hard condition satisfied. The hidden set and grader's seed are separate.

## Ranked work

1. **Increase useful coverage of the original pool.** This has the strongest project-specific rationale. The earlier matched 1,197-versus-1,000-crop comparison lowered memorization by 15.95 percentage points with similar FID, although both runs exceeded the cap and the class proportions differed slightly. Research also reports a greater tendency to memorize at smaller dataset sizes. A focused local pilot should target recoverable geometry errors and sources underrepresented by the current pool crops, using exact original photographs and only pool-side cues. Compare the number of visually suitable, distinct heads per reviewed candidate with the existing ranking. Keep the prior 63-crop holdout unchanged where possible. Require student approval and duplicate decisions on any additions before freezing a new manifest. Train from random initialization and compare at the same budget, sampler and seeds. A handful of new crops can establish pilot yield. A material expansion is needed to test model performance. [Gu et al., On Memorization in Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02664)

2. **Completed inference-only test on the known v002 checkpoint.** Tested DDIM with seeded per-image trajectory noise at eta 0.35 and 0.70, using the same initial noise, 50 steps, CFG 2.5, EMA weights and generation seed 0. Both passed trained CUDA prefix, label, microbatch and RNG checks and reproduced the old output exactly at eta zero. In the full 2,000-image official runs, eta 0.35 reached 90.80% accuracy, FID 76.47, recall 34.29% and 5.15% memorization. Eta 0.70 reached 92.00% accuracy, FID 78.44, recall 31.42% and 7.60% memorization. Both exceeded the hard cap and neither advanced. Preserve eta zero and the original export. [Song et al., DDIM, sections 4.1–4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.02502), [verified comparison](../experiments/v002_ddim_eta/comparison.json)

3. **Test one training redesign in parallel with personal crop review.** The sampler-only changes failed, and the eight additional v003 rows are too few for an informative quantity experiment. Test EDM-style noise preconditioning with the same 1,197 exact v002 crops, without the paper's image augmentations. Its fixed two-crop diagnostic passed with a 98.94% probe-MSE reduction. The [frozen training and evaluation plan](edm_32_seed17_63bc3fce35.json) uses the original small U-Net, 30,000 updates from random initialization, EMA, CFG 2.5, and 26 deterministic Heun noise levels. This yields 51 denoiser evaluations per guidance branch versus 50 for DDIM. The three declared checkpoints receive full seed-0 official grading, then the best valid improvement can advance to additional seeds. Published CIFAR-10 performance does not establish a gain on these crops. [Karras et al., EDM](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/a98846e9d9cc01cfb87eb694d946ce6b-Abstract.html)

The failed long training, velocity prediction, Min-SNR and tested endpoint/step changes remain comparison evidence. Keep development images and individual features inside the official evaluation boundary. Use neither additional training images nor pretrained generator weights. Preserve the exact reference export and its receipts.

The three official v002 evaluations also show a consistent class gap. Cat accuracy was 93.10–93.60% and dog accuracy was 85.60–86.70% at equal sample counts. On each seed, the dog class must improve by about 0.1–1.2 percentage points to reach 90% overall if the corresponding cat accuracy stays fixed. FID and recall still require much larger changes. Use the class gap as an aggregate diagnostic for future eligible pool coverage, with source-side evidence and personal decisions for every added crop. [v002 official receipts](../experiments/capacity_32_seed17_v002/comparison.json)

## Architecture and training review, November 13

| Approach | Published evidence | Decision for this project |
|---|---|---|
| Small pixel-space DDPM U-Net, epsilon loss, EMA, CFG, DDIM | DDPM/CFG/DDIM are established recipes. The 5.08M-parameter implementation passed three full official development checks at 11/15 each. | Retain as the comparator and first test for expanded reviewed data. The current 89.40–89.95% accuracy is close to the cutoff, while FID and recall need larger gains. |
| EDM-style pixel-space preconditioning | EDM reported class-conditional 32×32 CIFAR-10 results and separates network preconditioning, the noise distribution, and sampling. Its small-dataset recipe also used augmentation. | First architecture/training fallback. Use the existing U-Net, from scratch, with the same exact manifest and matched updates. No EDM weights or image augmentation. Treat the EDM parameterization, noise distribution, loss and compatible sampler as one recipe comparison. |
| Latent DiT and rectified flow | Original DiT used a pretrained Stable Diffusion VAE. Stable Diffusion 3 used scaled rectified-flow Transformers, and Qwen-Image-2.0 reports a multimodal diffusion Transformer with a language/vision encoder. These show current adoption in foundation image models. | Their published recipes operate at a different scale. Their pretrained components are ineligible here. A custom small pixel-space flow model could comply, but has weaker direct evidence for these crops and the 5% cap. |
| Pixel-space JiT | JiT trained directly on pixels without a tokenizer or pretraining and reported competitive ImageNet generation at 256 and 512 pixels. | A compliant research option if implemented within the caps. Both the patch design and data regime differ substantially from the 32×32 course task. Lower priority than a controlled EDM pilot. |
| EDM2 and restricted guidance | EDM2 reports gains from magnitude-preserving layers and adjustable EMA on ImageNet-512. Limited-interval guidance reports gains on ImageNet and SDXL. | Retain measured EMA and guidance for the matched EDM run. A late EMA reconstruction would need the necessary EMA tracks. Our existing interval screens give direct evidence for the particular v002 settings already tested. [EDM2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02696), [Limited-interval guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07724) |

No reliable adoption survey ranks these approaches for 1,197 reviewed crops, so the recommendation uses the task's evidence. Published FID values from unrelated datasets cannot be compared with course FID. Local trials showed that extra training or a newer loss can raise memorization before quality reaches the excellent tier. Keep seed 0/101/202 selection and untouched 303/404 final confirmation, promote only validated full evaluations, and report all measured failures. If the targeted recovery yields only a small addition, a from-scratch EDM diagnostic can test whether changing the denoising recipe has more value than continuing the same checkpoint.

Primary references: [Improved DDPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09672), [CFG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598), [EDM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364), [DiT and pretrained VAE](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.09748), [Rectified Flow Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03206), [Qwen-Image-2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10730), [Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747), [JiT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13720).

This cohort does not estimate overall pool precision. Do not scale this prompt alone.
