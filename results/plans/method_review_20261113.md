# Method review and experiment priorities

13 November 2026 · Design review for the local CISC3024 project

## Rubric and available evidence

The handout allows any class-conditional generator trained from random initialization on exact pool crops in the manifest. It requires offline deterministic generation and batch-composition isolation, a valid reconstructable manifest, at most 20M parameters and 100 MB, and at most 5% official memorization. A hard violation zeros the 15-mark evaluation. Accuracy, FID and recall each award up to five marks independently. Their Excellent thresholds are ≥90%, ≤50 and ≥40%. The hidden test and its seed remain independent of development selection. The separate data audit rewards agreement with graders on crop validity. The report, reproducibility, independent student checks and presentation have their own marks. Source: local `project_handout.pdf`, pp. 1–4.

The selected pixel U-Net uses 5,082,147 parameters and a 20,450,122-byte export. It predicts epsilon with a cosine schedule, class dropout and EMA; inference uses deterministic DDIM50 and CFG 2.5, where 1.0 is ordinary conditional prediction. Its three full official development scores are 11/15. Worst-seed gaps to Excellent are 0.60 percentage points of accuracy, 22.80 FID, and 9.80 percentage points of recall. Worst observed memorization leaves 0.40 percentage points of margin. The FID jump from the worst current 72.80 to the intermediate threshold of 60 earns one point, and 60 to 50 earns one more. Accuracy and recall each have one remaining point. The gap is therefore chiefly distribution quality and coverage, with a narrow memorization budget. [Three official v002 receipts](../experiments/capacity_32_seed17_v002/comparison.json)

The training data are nearly class-balanced. v002 has 1,197 approved crops and v004 has 1,221 (595 cats, 626 dogs), with identical 63 source/family-disjoint holdout rows. Official v002 cat accuracy was 93.10–93.60% and dog accuracy was 85.60–86.70%. This is an aggregate symptom, compatible with coverage differences or classifier confusion, and does not establish a class-count problem. In the 30k matched seed-0 comparison, v004 changed accuracy 89.40% → 88.10%, FID 72.80 → 74.42, recall 30.20% → 33.52%, and memorization 4.40% → 3.95%. The memorization comparison also changed the evaluator's rebuilt nearest-neighbour reference, so it does not isolate a change in the generator's tendency to copy. Future diagnostics may compare both generated sets against the same exact shared pool, with the official results still using each model's own full manifest. The 24 added crops and one training seed establish an observed tradeoff for this intervention. [Matched v004 receipts](../experiments/v004_coverage/screening.json) · [Geometry evidence](../curation/v004_review_geometry.json)

## Architecture assessment against primary literature

| Method | What transfers to this task | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel U-Net diffusion with CFG | Convolution and skips preserve spatial detail at 32×32. DDPM and Improved DDPM show low-resolution pixel-space generation, and CFG trades adherence against diversity with the same learned network. The course allows this network and the current export meets the caps with headroom. | Keep the exact v002 comparator. Preserve fixed physical execution batch 32. Current direct evidence outweighs scale-based comparisons. |
| EDM and EDM2 | Preconditioning, sigma distribution, solver and EMA are independently meaningful knobs. Published EDM CIFAR-10 results establish viability at 32×32. EDM2 training dynamics and EMA findings use a different setting. | Current EDM pilot scored 5/0/0; later checkpoints exceeded 5% memorization. Min-SNR and v-prediction also failed to improve the local reference. A full rewrite or longer run has low priority. |
| DiT, pixel patches and JiT | DiT establishes scalability in learned latent space; its published pretrained VAE conflicts with course restrictions. JiT establishes feasibility of direct clean-pixel prediction with patches, using ImageNet at 256/512. Patch packing preserves input values but changes local connectivity and processing resolution. | Matched 4×4 and 8×8 pixel patch models scored 2 and 1 at 30k with near-zero recall. Their result applies to the tested architecture/objective. Treat a scratch JiT/flow model as a separate high-cost design. |
| Small conditional GAN | StyleGAN2-ADA is genuine limited-data evidence and a scratch GAN can fit the interface. The published discriminator augmentation changes the real-image tensors it sees, so its recipe cannot be assumed compatible with this project's exact target rule. The published domains, resolution and metrics differ. | A distinct fallback with higher implementation and repeatability cost. Keep it behind the already compatible U-Net options. |

Primary sources: [DDPM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239), [Improved DDPM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/nichol21a.html), [CFG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.12598), [DDIM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02502), [EDM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364), [EDM2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02696), [DiT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09748), [JiT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13720), [StyleGAN2-ADA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06676). ## Order of operations

1. **Start with pool coverage and review yield.** The 1,280 already-screened sources are available for an exact visual pilot. Sample across pool-only appearance groups within species, tracking head pose, morphology and ear visibility, tonal range, backgrounds and duplicate families. Prioritize low-coverage dog appearances while retaining valid varied cats and difficult valid examples. Use only original sources and exact previews for decisions. Compare confirmed distinct crops per reviewer hour to the earlier shortlist. A planning target of roughly +120 distinct approved crops (about 10%) would provide a more useful contrast than +24. Treat it as a feasibility target and use observed yield to set the final budget. Keep the 63-row holdout and the exact parent rows. When the contrast is useful, train one matched fresh 30k run. If the screening yield is poor, change pool-side selection before committing student time or GPU time. [Diffusion memorization study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13188)
2. **Parallel low-cost test after the quota reset and before a new model family.** Screen one deterministic, second-order DPM-Solver++ sampler with the exact v002 epsilon checkpoint, converting its prediction to clean data at inference. Keep CFG, physical batch, and seeds fixed for the first comparison. Implement endpoint and output clipping deliberately, test prefix, label and RNG isolation, and retain DDIM50 as the control. Use a predeclared solver setting and full seed 0 before advancing. This probes integration error; the existing 100-step DDIM and endpoint experiments suggest modest upside. Stochastic DDIM eta variants already crossed 5% despite higher accuracy. The published limited-interval CFG results motivate the test family, but our endpoint/guidance comparison failed to advance, so avoid another broad guidance sweep. [DPM-Solver++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01095) · [Limited-interval guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07724) · [Local sampler evidence](../experiments/v002_sampler_comparison/comparison.json)
3. **Use matched replication before a model redesign.** Run the unchanged evaluator with 1,000 outputs per class. Apply the existing hard gate and the prespecified ranking to seed 0, then seeds 101 and 202 for a surviving candidate. These are generation seeds; they do not measure training-seed variance. For a material data-effect claim, repeat the parent/expanded pair from a second training initialization if feasible. Reserve seeds 303 and 404 for confirmation of the locked candidate. Record both the worst-seed score and distance from each rubric threshold, per-class accuracy, and all failed hard checks. Use course-only held-out crops for diagnostics and aggregate official metrics for selection. Complete the personal audit and independent verification on their proper deliverables.

No model was promoted and no experiment was launched for this design review.
